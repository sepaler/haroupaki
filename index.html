A hundred-year-old stone cottage above Megala Chorafia, entirely off the grid. It runs on sunlight. It sleeps two. The tree came first.

Scroll

What this is

Part of our grandmother Evanthia's house, still standing, still cool at noon.

No pool. No air conditioning. No washing machine. What there is instead: walls thick enough to keep the summer outside, a carob tree that has shaded this courtyard for a century, three beds of vegetables you are welcome to raid, and Wi-Fi — so you can be off-grid without falling off the map.

One day, roughly

Nothing is scheduled. It still goes like this.

07:40

The door opens onto the courtyard

You sleep well here because it is dark and there is no machine running anywhere. The light arrives before the heat does.

09:15

Coffee, under the tree, for a long time

The carob throws enough shade for the whole table. Most guests do not get further than this until eleven.

11:00

Down the gravel path, to see what is ready

Tomatoes, peppers, aubergines, courgettes. Rosemary, thyme and oregano all year. Prickly pears and grapes if you come late in the season.

15:30

The hottest part of the day, spent horizontally

Two chairs against the old wall. Aptera is five minutes away when you are ready to move; the sea is twenty.

19:45

Cooking outside, because the kitchen is outside

A gas burner, a built-in stone barbecue, a marble sink and a long counter facing the White Mountains.

23:10

Lanterns, and then the walk to bed

The bathroom is its own small building a few steps across the courtyard. You go outside to reach it, under whatever the sky is doing. People end up liking that part.

Before you book

Better said now than discovered later.

No air conditioning

Thick stone, deep shade, fans. Cool by day and cooler at night, but not a chilled box.

The bathroom is outside

Private and yours alone, a few steps across the courtyard. You do step out to reach it.

The kitchen is outside too

Covered and fully equipped, but open to the air. Bring a jumper for autumn dinners.

Solar means limits

Lights, fridge, Wi-Fi, charging: yes. Anything with a heating element: no.
